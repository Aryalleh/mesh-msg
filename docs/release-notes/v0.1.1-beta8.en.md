Board flasher for Android.

- New: mava-board-flasher-…-android.apk flashes the Mava board from a phone over a USB-OTG adapter, no computer needed. "Install everything" the first time (new identity and device password), "Update firmware only" later (keeps identity and settings). Every region is checked against the board's own checksum before it restarts.
- "Board log" in the same app restarts the board and shows what it prints, with the hotspot name and device password picked out at the top.
- Works with the ESP32-S3's own USB and with boards that have a CP210x or CH340 USB chip.
- The board firmware itself is unchanged from 0.1.1-beta7. It has still not been tested on real hardware.
