Fix: the board said "relay no wifi" although its Wi-Fi was joined.

- The relay status was only updated after a connection attempt ended. On a network that silently drops the relay, an attempt could take minutes (a secure connection waited up to 120 s for the relay to answer), so the log kept the old "no wifi". Now the log says "connecting (address)" while it tries, then the real reason it failed. Attempts give up after 8 s (connect) and 20 s (secure handshake), then the board tries the relay's IP address.
- When the relay is off, the log says why: no Wi-Fi saved, switched off in the setup panel, or no relay address.
- Update the board with "Update firmware only" (Mava Flasher downloads it from GitHub). This keeps the board's account and contacts. Still not tested on real hardware.
