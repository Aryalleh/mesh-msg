Fix: the board restarted by itself (watchdog) while a phone sent it many packets.

- Board: every packet the board stored was written to flash at once. A phone that passes its relay traffic to the board over Bluetooth sends ten or more packets a second, and the constant flash writes stalled the board's other processor core until the watchdog restarted it ("task_wdt: IDLE0"). The board now notes the changes and writes at most one packet every quarter second.
- Board log, account line: shows the board account's ID, and the last accounts that messages were heard for ("messages heard for other accounts: …"). If you send a message from the phone and it appears there instead of under "messages for it", the phone is writing to an old account of this board: delete the board contact on the phone and add it again from the board's "My contact" QR code.
- Update with Mava Flasher, "Update firmware only" (it keeps the account). Still not tested on real hardware.
