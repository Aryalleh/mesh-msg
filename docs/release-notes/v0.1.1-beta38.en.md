Board screen layout: a menu for a phone-shaped screen, and call video on the whole screen.

- Home menu: when the screen is upright (portrait, like a phone in the hand) each of the four items has its own full-width row; on its side (landscape) they stay two per row.
- Calls: Mute and Hang up are now in the top bar next to Home, so the other side's video fills the rest of the screen. The bar shows who you are talking to and the call time.
- The call's security code is behind a new key (eye) next to Hang up: tap it and the code pops up to read out and compare, and it closes by itself after 10 seconds.
- An incoming call still shows Answer and Decline in the middle of the screen.
- Update the board with Mava Board ("Update firmware only" keeps the account).
