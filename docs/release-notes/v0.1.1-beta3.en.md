Third beta of Mava.

- New: profile pictures. Set yours in Settings (Android) or ⚙ Settings → You (desktop). Your contacts see it when they open your chat. It travels only over a direct connection between you and them, never through relays or the mesh, and only when they ask for it.
- New: chat folders. Tabs above the chat list: All, Personal, Groups, Unread, plus your own folders. On Android, long-press a chat to put it in a folder; on desktop use ✎.
- Desktop: a new Telegram-like look (blue, with light and dark following your system). The old look is still in Settings → Appearance.
- Android: the "Mava is running" notification is now silent and minimal (no status-bar icon, hidden on the lock screen). Settings → Mesh explains how to switch it off completely; Mava keeps running.
- Everything from 0.1.1-beta2: update check, always-on mesh, calls showing peer-to-peer or relay.

Coming next: video calls, calls on desktop, group calls. Not yet tested on real phones: Bluetooth mesh and USB LoRa. Report problems at https://github.com/Aryalleh/mesh-msg/issues
