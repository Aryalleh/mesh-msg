Relays without a server (Cloudflare, ArvanCloud), one installer, and trips with a map.

- New: a relay can run on Cloudflare Workers or ArvanCloud Edge Computing, with no server. It carries messages, works with gateways, syncs with other relays, has the admin panel and runs trips. Media files and the UDP services for calls need a server relay.
- One installer for every kind: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aryalleh/mesh-msg/main/server/deploy/install-relay.sh | bash` asks whether the relay runs on this server, on Cloudflare or on Arvan.
- Trips: leaders see every member on a map in the app (tracks, who is lost, who stopped sharing). Map tiles need internet once; they stay on the phone after that.
- "I'm lost" also sends your position to the trip's leaders as a message and opens that chat, so you can keep writing.
- Send your location in any chat (attach → Location). It shows as a card: Navigate asks which navigation app to use, and there are Google Maps, Neshan and Balad buttons.
- The admin panel shows the relay's trip with a link to its map.
- Update the Android apps; update the trip's relay too.
