Video calls.

- New: video calls on Android. Tap the camera button in a chat; the other phone shows "Incoming video call". During any call you can turn the camera on or off and switch between front and back camera; your own picture shows in the corner. Like voice, video goes only peer to peer, never through a relay (except a relay's own admins).
- Video adapts to the connection: quality drops when packets are lost and recovers when the line is clean. On mobile data the call screen notes that video uses about 3–15 MB a minute.
- Calls with older versions still work, as voice.
- Downloads from a nearby device retry after 10 seconds instead of 2 minutes when the first try fails.
- First release with video: please report how it works on your phone at https://github.com/Aryalleh/mesh-msg/issues
