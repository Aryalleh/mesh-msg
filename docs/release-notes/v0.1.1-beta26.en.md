Fix: messages over Bluetooth between a phone and the board were lost.

- Board: everything a phone writes over Bluetooth passes through a queue to the board's main loop. The queue had 32 places, and when it was full the board threw writes away without a word. A phone that passes its relay traffic to the board writes several packets a second, so pieces were lost. One lost piece shifts the byte stream: every packet after it was garbage and rejected, your messages included, until the board dropped the link ("last drop 0x216"). A lost "subscribed" or "disconnected" event also left the link half dead after a reconnect (the in/out counters stopped). Now the queue has 256 places in PSRAM, and nothing is dropped: when it is full, the board answers the phone's write a little later, so the phone slows down.
- Board log: the Bluetooth line shows "queue N waiting, lost N, corrupt streams N" (both should stay 0), and the relay line shows packets sent up to the relay and taken from it.
- Update with Mava Flasher, "Update firmware only" (keeps the account). Still not tested on real hardware.
