Fix: the phone and the board never joined in one Bluetooth mesh.

- The phone does send every message on every path at once: relay, LoRa board and every Bluetooth peer. The problem was that the Bluetooth link to the board never formed.
- Board: a phone that once paired with the board in the pairing-code builds (beta10 to beta13) still keeps that pairing. After a fresh flash the board no longer has the matching keys, so the phone's encryption attempt failed and the link dropped at once. The board now has its own Bluetooth address, made from its identity, so the phone sees it as a new device and no old pairing gets in the way.
- Android: before connecting to a mesh peer or the board, the app removes such an old pairing where Android allows it. If the board's address changed, the app finds the board again by itself.
- The board log now has a "Bluetooth" line every 10 seconds: the address, whether advertising is on, the links, connects, hellos and drops, and why the last link dropped.
- Tip: if your phone's Bluetooth settings list "Mava-…" under paired devices, tap Forget.
- Update both: the Android app, and the board with Mava Flasher ("Update firmware only"). Still not tested on real hardware.
