The handheld is a messenger of its own.

- The Mava handheld (and a board shown on a phone with Mava Screen) now sends and receives messages itself, without a phone: its own identity, contacts and conversations, end-to-end encrypted exactly like the apps, over LoRa, the Bluetooth mesh and the relay.
- Chats → "My contact" shows a QR code: scan it in the Mava app (Add contact) to write to the handheld. People nearby appear in Chats by themselves, by the name they announce.
- Sent messages get a ✓ when the other side has received them. Long messages are split over several LoRa frames and joined again.
- Conversations are kept on the device across restarts.
- Install with Mava Flasher: "Update firmware only" keeps everything else. Still not tested on real hardware.
