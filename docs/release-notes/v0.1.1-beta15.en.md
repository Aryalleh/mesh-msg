One Bluetooth mesh for every device.

- Mava boards now connect to each other over Bluetooth too, not only over LoRa. A board with the higher session ID connects to the other one by itself.
- The Linux desktop (meshd) joins the Bluetooth mesh: it finds phones and Mava boards nearby and connects to them (up to 4), so a PC with Bluetooth carries their messages to relays and LoRa. On by default; `[bluetooth] off = true` turns it off. It needs a Bluetooth adapter and BlueZ.
- Together with 0.1.1-beta14: phone ↔ phone, iPhone ↔ Android, phone ↔ board, board ↔ board and PC → everyone, all in one mesh, with no pairing.
- Still not tested on real hardware.
