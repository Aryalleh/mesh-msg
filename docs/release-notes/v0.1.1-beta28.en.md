Fix: after one Bluetooth hiccup, the phone and the board broke their link again and again.

- When the phone dropped the Bluetooth link to the board and reconnected at once, Android reused the old link while it was still closing. The board then saw the phone's new greeting (hello) in the middle of the old data and called the stream corrupt. Each reconnect broke again ("frame length 5080217": 0x4D is the greeting). Now:
  - Board: a hello again from the same phone (or at a packet boundary) starts a fresh session on that connection, and the half-sent rest of the old stream is thrown away. The log counts these ("restarted N").
  - Android: after dropping a Bluetooth peer, the app waits 5 seconds before connecting to it again, so the old link closes first.
- Android, Network screen: the Bluetooth card shows why the last peer went away ("Last disconnect: …"). Please include it in reports.
- Update both: the Android app, and the board with Mava Flasher, "Update firmware only" (keeps the account). Still not tested on real hardware.
