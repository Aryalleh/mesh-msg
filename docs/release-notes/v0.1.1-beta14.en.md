The Mava board joins the phones' Bluetooth mesh, with no pairing code.

- Phones running Mava now find the board by themselves through their Bluetooth mesh, the same way they find each other: no button and no pairing code. Messages from phones nearby go out over the board's LoRa radio and its internet relay, and what the board hears comes back to them. Up to 3 phones at once.
- No more Bluetooth passkey. Because anyone nearby can now connect, a phone over Bluetooth can only send and receive messages through the board; its settings, Wi-Fi, restart and wipe stay on USB and the setup panel.
- "Connect board over Bluetooth" in the app still works, without pairing, and shows the board's radio status.
- More free memory on the board: its message store moved to PSRAM, leaving about 80 kB more internal memory for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the secure relay connection.
- Still not tested on real hardware.
