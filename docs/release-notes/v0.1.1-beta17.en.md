Landscape screen, a much faster screen on the phone, and the board relays between all layers.

- The handheld screen is now landscape, 320 × 240, like a BlackBerry: more room, easier to read. The home screen shows its buttons in two columns, and the touch keyboard leaves the chat visible.
- Mava Screen over USB is much faster. The board was sending at most 256 bytes per loop to USB (a small default buffer and one chunk per loop), so a full screen took seconds; now it uses a 16 kB buffer and sends everything waiting each time.
- Mava Screen has a switch "Type with the phone's keyboard" (on by default): the board hides its touch keyboard and you type with your phone. One firmware works with the Pico keyboard module (own keypad, BlackBerry 9900 keyboard, with or without trackpad), the touch keyboard and the phone's keyboard.
- The board relays between every layer: what a Bluetooth phone sends goes out on LoRa, to the relay and to the other phones; what it hears on LoRa reaches the phones and the relay; what comes from the relay reaches the phones and LoRa. Now checked by a test.
- Update with Mava Flasher ("Update firmware only") and install the new Mava Screen. Still not tested on real hardware.
