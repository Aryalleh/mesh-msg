The Mava app talks to the Mava board over Bluetooth.

- New: Network → "Connect board over Bluetooth". The app finds the nearest Mava board, pairs with it (Android asks for the board's 6-digit Bluetooth passkey, shown in the board's log or on its screen) and uses its LoRa radio and relay without a cable. It reconnects by itself when the board comes back in range or restarts.
- The "nearby" Bluetooth mesh is for phones only: it never finds the board, which is why it kept searching. The board connection is the new button above.
- Board flasher: when the board doesn't answer a Wi-Fi or hotspot command, the error now says what came back over USB (nothing, only log text, or other frames), so the cause can be found.
- Still not tested on real hardware.
