Video on the board's calls, a faster screen, Back and Home on the touch screen, and a choice of two firmwares.

- Video in board calls: the board shows the other side's camera. It sends no picture itself. A video call from a phone is answered with the picture on the board's screen, and in a voice call with the board the phone can turn its camera on at any time. Phones send the board small JPEG frames (MJPEG, about 10 a second); between phones calls stay on H.264. Update the Mava app on the phones for this.
- Fixed: Mava Screen's keys and audio stopped working. The board's USB receive buffer overflowed while the phone sent its microphone; it is now 32 times bigger.
- Fixed: the display was slow in beta33. Its drawing buffers are back in fast memory.
- Touch only: without a keyboard, the top bar has Back and Home buttons (and a button back to a call in progress) in place of the long connection text.
- Two firmwares in every pre-release: with the phone simulators (Mava Screen as screen, keys, speaker and microphone) or without them, faster, for a board with its own display and keys. Mava Flasher has a switch "Phone simulators (Mava Screen)"; on Linux, `mava-flash firmware --plain`.
- Update the board with Mava Flasher ("Update firmware only" keeps the account). Not yet tested on real hardware.
