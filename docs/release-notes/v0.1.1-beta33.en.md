Touch fixed and calibratable on the board's display, and a call button in its chats.

- Fixed: touches were still mirrored top to bottom. Both axes of the panel now run the right way for the red "TFT SPI 240x320 V1.2" module.
- New: Settings → Calibrate touch. Tap the centre of three yellow crosses and the board saves a map for your panel (mirrored, rotated or swapped axes all work). Use it if a touch still lands in the wrong place.
- New: a 📞 Call button at the top of every chat on the board, so calls work with touch alone (the 📞 key does the same).
- Calls on the board as in beta32: voice, on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet; turn on the speaker and microphone in Mava Screen while the audio module is missing.
- Update the board with Mava Flasher ("Update firmware only" keeps the account).
