Trips: location sharing for a group trip, and a board setting for screens that show red as blue.

- New: Trips (Settings → Trips). For a group trip without roaming, e.g. a walk where someone can get lost: a leader starts a trip on the group's relay (only the relay's admins can), and members' phones ask whether to share their position with the trip's leaders. If they agree, the phone sends its position every few minutes, encrypted so only the leaders can read it, over Bluetooth to nearby phones, over LoRa and through the relay, even without internet. A notification shows while sharing, with a Stop button.
- Members: "I'm lost" sends the position every minute and alerts the leaders; "Stop sharing" at any time (the leaders see it); sharing ends by itself when the trip ends.
- Leaders: see each member's last position, distance and direction, battery and time; open it in a map app; ask a member for their position; add other leaders (admins of the relay); show a QR code for members; a key for the map in the relay's admin panel (/admin/trip), where positions are opened in the leader's browser and the relay never sees them.
- The trip's relay needs this version too.
- Board: if the call video on the board looks blue (and the red Hang up key looks blue), Settings → "Swap red and blue". The choice is saved on the board.
- Update the board with Mava Board ("Update firmware only" keeps the account), and the Android apps.
