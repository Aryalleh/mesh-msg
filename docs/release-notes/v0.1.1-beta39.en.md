Board: a setting for screens that show red as blue.

- If the call video on the board looks blue (and the red Hang up key looks blue), the screen module wants red and blue the other way round. Settings now shows red, green and blue swatches; if "red" looks blue, tap "Swap red and blue". The choice is saved on the board.
- Update the board with Mava Board ("Update firmware only" keeps the account).
