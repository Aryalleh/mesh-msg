One app for the board, "Ringing" when you call, and screen rotation on the board.

- Mava Board: the flasher and Mava Screen are now one app with two tabs, "Flash & set up" and "Screen, keys, sound" (the second in pre-releases only). It installs over Mava Flasher; uninstall Mava Screen. The tabs take turns with the board's USB.
- Calling: once the other phone (or board) rings, your screen says "Ringing…" instead of "Calling…" and you hear a ringback tone; before that the call is still on its way. On phones and on the board. Both sides need this version for it.
- Board Settings → Rotation: landscape, portrait, or either upside down. Touch, touch calibration and the picture in the Screen tab follow it.
- Update the phones' Mava app, install Mava Board, and update the board ("Update firmware only" keeps the account). Not yet tested on real hardware.
