One account on the device: the iPhone and the handheld are the same messenger.

- The browser app on a Mava board no longer makes a separate account. After the device password it shows the board's own account: the same identity as the handheld screen and its "My contact" QR code, the same chats and history. Send from the iPhone, read on the handheld, or the other way round.
- Carry the board in a backpack and use the phone as its screen; if the phone dies, the handheld has everything.
- The board keeps its own hotspot (Mava-XXXX) on while it is connected to your Wi-Fi, so the phone can join the board directly (http://192.168.4.1/app/) while the board stays online.
- A board without a screen is an account too: the browser is its screen.
- Change the default device password (12345678): it now protects the hotspot and the account.
- Still not tested on real hardware.
