Calls like a phone call, read receipts, storage manager.

- Calls: an incoming call shows Answer and Decline right on the notification and rings full screen over the lock screen. On Android 14 and newer the app asks once for the full-screen permission; it is also in Settings while it is off.
- During a voice call, tap ⌄ or Back to use the rest of the app: a green bar at the top shows the call time, tap it to return. The notification shows the running time and Hang up.
- Bluetooth headsets: a connected headset is used for calls automatically, also when it connects during a call. The audio button lets you pick headset, phone or speaker.
- Read receipts: a colored second tick (✓✓) when the other person has opened the chat. They go only through relays, never over Bluetooth or LoRa. You can turn them off in Settings.
- Storage: Settings → Storage lists the files on this device per chat and type, and deletes them by chat, type or date range. The messages stay.
- If a contact lost their account and now uses a new one, open the old chat → ⋮ → Merge into new account: the whole history moves to the new chat.
- Tap a contact's photo in the chat to see it full size and zoom in. Profile pictures are now sharper.
- Desktop: read receipts, storage, merging chats and the full-size photo too.
